What You Should Know

  • Customization: All pricing is flexible. Dietary requirements, menu complexity, and special requests may adjust pricing.
  • Minimums: Private dinner events have a 4-person minimum. Event catering for 20+ guests.
  • Venue: Services provided at your home or private venue in the Roaring Fork Valley (Aspen, Basalt, Snowmass, Carbondale).
  • Seasonal Pricing: Holiday events and peak season (summer/fall) may have premium rates. Advance booking recommended.
  • Booking: 2–4 week advance notice for most events; custom quotes available for expedited requests.
  • Travel: Local to Roaring Fork Valley. Travel outside valley available; mileage and time fees apply.
