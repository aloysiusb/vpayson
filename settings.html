Making it permanent

What you set here is saved in this browser only. Every page of the site will wear it on this machine, and nobody else sees any of it. That is deliberate — it means you can try things on the live site without publishing a half-finished idea.
To make a setting real for visitors, press Copy the CSS and paste the block over the matching lines in the :root of index.html, work/evergreen/index.html and settings.html — then commit. Those three pages each hold their own copy of the tokens on purpose, so each stands alone as one file.
